# Code of Conduct

## P8OS — Open Cognitive Infrastructure for Autonomous Systems (OCI4AS)

---

## Our Purpose

P8OS is an open research and engineering initiative dedicated to advancing the infrastructure for future autonomous intelligent systems.

Our community values:

* curiosity;
* scientific thinking;
* engineering excellence;
* constructive collaboration;
* openness to new ideas.

We welcome contributors from different backgrounds, disciplines and experience levels who share these principles.

---

# Expected Behavior

We encourage every participant to:

* communicate respectfully and professionally;
* focus discussions on technical and scientific topics;
* provide constructive feedback;
* support new contributors;
* document ideas clearly;
* accept evidence-based discussion;
* acknowledge mistakes and improve through collaboration.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within the project:

* personal attacks;
* harassment or intimidation;
* discrimination;
* abusive language;
* deliberate disruption of discussions;
* trolling;
* spam;
* publication of confidential information without permission;
* intentionally misleading technical information.

Disagreements are expected.

Personal hostility is not.

---

# Research Principles

P8OS is a research-oriented project.

Contributors should distinguish between:

* verified implementation;
* experimental concepts;
* hypotheses;
* future research ideas.

Speculation should always be clearly identified as such.

---

# Open Collaboration

P8OS encourages:

* respectful technical debate;
* alternative architectural proposals;
* reproducible research;
* transparent engineering decisions.

Good ideas are welcome regardless of where they originate.

---

# Intellectual Property

Contributors should submit only work that they have the right to contribute.

Respect:

* copyrights;
* licenses;
* patents;
* third-party intellectual property.

---

# Communication

Technical discussions should remain:

* objective;
* evidence-based;
* solution-oriented.

When disagreements occur, contributors are encouraged to discuss ideas rather than individuals.

---

# Project Independence

P8OS is developed as an independent open-source initiative.

Technical decisions should prioritize:

* long-term maintainability;
* architectural consistency;
* platform independence;
* openness.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior repeatedly violates this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions should be fair, transparent and proportional.

---

# Our Goal

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is not to limit discussion.

Its purpose is to create an environment where researchers and engineers can work together respectfully while building the next generation of intelligent system infrastructure.
